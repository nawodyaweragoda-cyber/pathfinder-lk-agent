# Ella

Ella is a hill-country town in the Badulla District popular with backpackers.
Key attractions: Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adams Peak (easy 45-min hike),
Ella Rock (harder 4-hour hike), and Ravana Falls.

- The Kandy to Ella train is one of the most scenic rides in the world;
  reserve seats in advance during December to March high season.
- Best months: January to March (dry, clear views)
- Nine Arch Bridge: mid-morning train crossings are the most photographed.
