# Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Sigiriya is a 5th-century rock fortress in the Matale District, built by King Kashyapa.
It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its frescoes, the Mirror Wall, and the
Lion Gate. The climb involves roughly 1,200 steps and takes 1.5 to 2 hours round trip.

- Best time to climb: 7.00 am opening, to avoid heat and crowds
- Ticket (foreigners): approx. USD 35; SAARC discount available
- Nearby: Pidurangala Rock (cheaper alternative viewpoint), Dambulla Cave Temple (30 min)
- Getting there: bus from Dambulla, or tuk-tuk from Habarana / Sigiriya village
