# Yala National Park

Yala, in the southeast, has one of the highest leopard densities in the world.
Block 1 is the most popular safari zone.

- Safari times: 6.00 am and 3.00 pm game drives; morning drives have better sightings
- Season: February to July is best; the park may close partially in September (drought)
- Wildlife: leopards, elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles, peacocks
- Base towns: Tissamaharama or Kirinda; jeep hire is arranged through hotels
