# Kandy

Kandy is the cultural capital of Sri Lanka and home of the Temple of the Sacred
Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa). The Esala Perahera festival (July/August) is one
of Asias grandest processions.

- Temple dress code: shoulders and knees covered; shoes removed at entrance
- Other sights: Kandy Lake, Peradeniya Royal Botanical Gardens, Bahirawakanda Buddha
- Kandy is the usual starting point for the scenic train to Nanu Oya and Ella
