# Galle Fort

Galle Fort is a 17th-century Dutch colonial fortification on the south coast,
a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walking the ramparts at sunset is the classic activity.

- Highlights: Lighthouse, Dutch Reformed Church, Maritime Museum, boutique cafes
- Getting there: Southern Expressway from Colombo (approx. 1.5 hours by car),
  or the coastal train from Colombo Fort station
- Nearby beaches: Unawatuna (10 min), Jungle Beach, Mirissa (45 min) for whale
  watching November to April
